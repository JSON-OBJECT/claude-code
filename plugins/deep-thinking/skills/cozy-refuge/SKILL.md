# Cozy Refuge

Self-contained distillation of a cozy-refuge mood canon (verified against Project Horseshoe's *Coziness in Games*, Michael Bull's *Sound Moves*, Miyazaki's *ma* interview, Kazumi Totaka's Animal Crossing sound philosophy, Roger Linn's swing numbers, and lo-fi/ambience community consensus). Everything you need is in this file — no external doc required.

**One-line definition.** *Cozy refuge is the aesthetic — equal parts comfort and thin wistfulness — that arises when you place a semi-permeable membrane between an outside world that demands nothing and an inside marked by traces of care.* The girl in the sunlit café and the survivor in the fallout bunker are two notations of the same fantasy.

## The Iron Law

```
NO COZY WITHOUT A MEMBRANE, A CARE-TRACE, AND ZERO OBLIGATION.
No membrane = exposure. No care-trace = liminal drift. A single obligation anywhere = not this mood.
```

## Core Axioms (all mandatory — not a menu)

1. **Cozy = the fantasy of "safety, abundance, softness"** (Project Horseshoe 2017). Threat is NOT a required ingredient — what is required is *a sign that the outside demands nothing of you*. The bunker notates that sign with walls; the sunlit café notates it with light and bread (two notations of one fantasy). When you do use threat, it must stay strictly **beyond the frame** (out the window, on the radio, in lore text) — the moment it intrudes, you have horror.
2. **The membrane**: every scene gets exactly one semi-permeable membrane between self and world — headphones (the *auditory bubble*), window + rain, night, train glass, time, the end of the world. The world must remain *visible and audible but unable to enter*. Full seal = claustrophobia; no membrane = exposure.
3. **Ma (間) and pooled time**: remove clocks, deadlines, and counters; tell time only with season, weather, and hour of day. Loop = pooled eternity. "No sound that is unnecessary" (Totaka). Empty stretches where nothing happens are the mood's breathing.

**Polarity test** — the same raw materials (empty rooms, no people, nostalgia) collapse into liminal dread unless three switches are set: ① **enclosure + warmth** (lamp, blanket, low ceiling — never fluorescent lighting or endless corridors) ② **care-traces** (objects mid-use: a steaming mug, a half-read book — never a showroom of untouched things) ③ **companion signal** (a cat, an absorbed figure whose gaze never meets the camera, a voice on the radio — never fully unpeopled AND lifeless).

**Emotional blend**: comfort + calm + longing + *a thin wistfulness* (mandatory ingredient — reflective nostalgia, mono no aware). Comfort must always hold the majority. ⚠️ Heuristic ratio: comfort 5 : calm 3 : longing 1.5 : wistfulness 0.5; darker modes raise longing/wistfulness, but the moment wistfulness takes the majority you pass through mallsoft into liminal.

## The Six Modes (swap only the membrane, key light, and sound)

| ④ | **Moving refuge** | speed + glass | scenery streaming past the train window, tunnel flicker | seat, head resting on the window, earphones | rail-joint rhythm, wipers, engine hum = natural loops | letting-go, travel melancholy (旅愁) | the sea-train scene in *Spirited Away* · train-window channels |
| ⑤ | **Melancholy loss** (mallsoft / jazz kissa) | time | aged fluorescent / department-store indirect light, warmed | fountain, escalator, LPs, old magazines | reverb-drenched mall jazz + heavy vinyl noise | longing dialed up — boundary mode, handle with care | 猫シ Corp. *Palm Mall* |

⚠️ **Mode budget** (same shape as earned-juice budgeting): high-frequency surfaces (a screen seen daily) take the early modes ①–②; special moments (first run, year-end, campaign) take the late modes ⑤–⑥. Overusing the strong modes causes inflation.

## Visual Grammar

- **Composition**: frame-within-frame (window, door frame, bookshelf gap — wrap the scene once more; 2+ screen edges closed by interior structure). One **anchor furniture** (bed / chair / desk) as the eye's landing point — no plaza compositions; go *narrow and deep*. **Three-plane depth**: foreground (prop detail) / midground (anchor + figure) / background (the world beyond the membrane — city, ruins). **Figure rule**: a single absorbed person with **averted gaze** (profile view — companionship without the burden of being observed); the moment the figure looks at the camera, the body double becomes a watcher. If unpeopled, substitute **just-left traces** (a still-steaming mug, a radio left on, a dented cushion).
- **Light**: **single key light** — exactly one of lamp / window / monitor / candle dominates. Design the gap: warm interior (amber) vs cool beyond-the-membrane (dusk violet, ruin gray-blue) — the color-temperature gap IS the size of the comfort (⚠️ interior ≈ 2700–3500 K vs a 2000 K+ delta outside). No multi-source light, no uniform flat light, no direct noon sun — always scattered, indirect, local; the hour of day picks the light (see mode table).
- **Objects = lived-in feel** (生活感 *seikatsukan*): fill the room with *traces of use*, not decoration — a folded page corner, a half-drunk cup, a blanket tossed over a chair, slippers kicked off (the Unpacking principle: objects tell the owner's story). **Warmth cues ≥ 2** (steam, lamp/candle glow, blanket, textiles) **+ companion cue ≥ 1** (cat, plant, radio on). Food appears at turning points of care, never as decoration (Ghibli grammar: Haku's onigiri, Howl's bacon-and-eggs). The only things that move: rising steam, curtains, drifting dust motes, a cat's tail, the rain outside — figures stay at breath / page-turn level.
- **Texture** ⚠️: film grain 3–6% opacity · saturation 20–45% · hand-drawn warmth (watercolor/cel blend — avoid pure vector and photoreal CG) · seamless loop 4–12 s (cut at the frame of least motion) · rain in 2–3 layers with parallax speed differences · no camera movement (up to ±2% micro-parallax). Regional note: for Korean audiences, live-action **no-talk vlog** texture ("the scratch of a pencil filling the room instead of words") performs the same psychological function as an animation loop.
- **Web-UI tokens (worked example)**: dusk palette `#1A1626` room dark / `#2A2138` furniture / `#FFB566` lamp amber / `#D97A8E` sunset rose / `#EDE4D8` warm-paper text · SVG `feTurbulence` grain overlay at 3–6% · diagonal rain layer via `repeating-linear-gradient` (linear drift animation) · `radial-gradient` lamp glow with irregular flicker keyframes · vignette (radial edge darkening) · serif display face (warmth) × sans-serif body pairing. `prefers-reduced-motion` support is mandatory (stop rain and flicker).

## Audio Grammar (equal pillar — in this genre, the music invented the visuals)

- **Music**: BPM **60–90** (production-community consensus; tolerated ceiling 100) · swing **54–58%** (Roger Linn: 54% = slightly loose straight, 58% = "very loose and natural swing"; valid range 50–70%) **or quantize OFF** (J Dilla technique: kick/snare on grid, hats rushed/dragged per hit; velocity randomization + ghost notes to fake the human hand) · extended **7th/9th jazz chords** (e.g., a minor-key VI–i–V7 with tensions) · Rhodes piano, felt piano, muted electric guitar · sample sources: old jazz/soul vinyl, spoken-word records, anime dialogue, field recordings · effect chain: **vinyl crackle** (straight onto the drum bus) → **low-pass ~3.1 kHz**, gentle slope → clean up sub-noise below 20 Hz → **tape saturation + wow & flutter** (micro pitch wobble) → low-mid boost + parallel compression. Structure: 1–3 min, **no climax — sustain the vibe** (intro → gradual loop variation → breakdown); avoid musical expression tilted toward any other emotion (Totaka's prohibition). **Imperfection (crackle) IS the warmth** — the exact same function as visual grain.
- **Ambience — three layers**: **Background** = the threat at a distance (far thunder at ~3 s of delay per km, ruin wind, city hum — high-cut = distance) / **Midground** = the membrane's own sounds (rain on glass, rain on a metal roof, wind, rail joints) / **Foreground** = care & companion cues (page turns, pencil, teacup, cat purr, radio voice). **Distance = low-pass**: to push a threat farther away, shave its highs. **Loops must contain variation** — an unvarying ambient loop breaks immersion the moment it is recognized. Classic foley recipes: frying bacon = rain; rainstick → soda fizz = rain starting → settling in; cellophane = fire. Cozy-apocalypse variant: over the dark-ambient bed, the foreground belongs to **human-trace sounds** (radio chatter, shortwave, a periodic beep) — the "someone is still out there" survival signal.
- **Levels (never mix norms)**: ambience bed **−23 to −32 LUFS** · **no heavy compression** (dynamics ARE the realism) · music sits low over the bed like air (≈ −20 dB region). **−14 LUFS is a music-streaming normalization norm — push an ambience master up to −14 and the mood dies.** ⚠️ Blend heuristic: music : membrane sounds : life-sounds ≈ 4 : 3 : 1, with Background kept just above the threshold of feeling ("felt, not heard").
- **Everyday-sound coordinates** (ASMR-trigger mapping, Barratt & Davis 2015): the cozy quadrant is **crisp + repetitive-task** sounds — page turns, pencil, keyboard, tea pouring. **Whispering and personal-attention triggers are FORBIDDEN** (their relational intimacy pierces the membrane). **No ticking clocks** (clock time intruding). Category note: rain/fireplace recordings are soundscape / field-recording material, not ASMR.
- **Proof points (games)**: Animal Crossing — the opening theme's melody is re-woven through every hour's music and even the sound effects (world coherence), and the acoustic guitar was chosen because "anyone can play it with a few chords" = familiarity = relaxation. Unpacking — 14,000+ foley recordings across item × surface pairs, so each placement is a tactile reward. Spirit City — a user-mixable 12-layer soundscape editor (the three-layer framework, productized).

## Copy Grammar

Tagline template: juxtapose the threat and the warmth around a comma — the *Girls' Last Tour* grammar, 「__と、仲良く。」 / "With __, on friendly terms." (the original catch copy: 「絶望と、仲良く。」 — "With despair, on friendly terms.") Micro-narrative in 1–2 sentences that states the contrast explicitly (*"Day 326 of the resistance. Today's rain brings calm."*). Time is written as "a rainy Tuesday afternoon" — never dates, never deadlines. **Threat substitution**: when transplanting to a modern product, the ruins beyond the wall are a swappable slot — put notifications, deadlines, noise, and timelines outside the membrane instead ("a bunker in the age of noise").

## Lineage (why the genre looks and sounds like this)

Three rivers converge. **(A) Japan's post-1995 healing culture**: the Kobe earthquake + the Aum attack + the Lost Decade made *calm itself a commodity* (healing / iyashi culture — scholarly anchor: Paul Roquet, *Ambient Media: Japanese Atmospheres of Self*, 2016, on media consumed as "sensory resources for personal mood regulation"; background frame: Hiroki Azuma's collapse of grand narratives and Tsunehiro Uno's diagnosis that a disillusioned generation was offered "the exact opposite of reality — a fun, peaceful everyday"). *Nichijō-kei* branches off with Azumanga Daioh (1999), *iyashikei* is defined by healing effect, the apocalyptic variant *shūmatsu nichijō-kei* gets its urtext in *Yokohama Kaidashi Kikou* (1994) — "a post-apocalypse that isn't scary; humanity sunsetting gracefully" — and the lived-practice branch *teinei na kurashi* takes *Little Forest* as its image canon. **(B) The music lineage**: 90s boom-bap/jazz-rap (A Tribe Called Quest) → J Dilla (the third time-feel; quantize off) and Nujabes (the melodic repackager and international spreader of jazz-loop hip hop via *Samurai Champloo*, 2004 — not a lone genesis; note also that there is NO direct Nujabes↔Ghibli link — the Ghibli connection belongs to the visual lineage only, so keep the two threads separate) → ChilledCow (2015) → a copyright accident fixes the genre's look: the first stream looped studying Shizuku from Ghibli's *Whisper of the Heart* (1995) without license → takedown → in 2018 Juan Pablo Machado is commissioned to "keep it Ghibli-like" → Jade (the window view is real Lyon) → renamed **Lofi Girl** (2021; a 13,000-hour continuous stream) → **Synthwave Boy** (2023; the family splits into time-of-day SKUs). **So lo-fi girl is literally a Ghibli girl's direct descendant, and this is the rare genre where the music invented the visuals.** **(C) The space-psychology lineage**: Appleton's prospect-refuge (1975) → hygge physiology → Project Horseshoe's cozy definition (2017) → the pandemic cottagecore / Animal Crossing boom → the cozy-games market (Steam tag: 146 new titles in 2023 → 371 in 2024) → NYT mainstream canonization ("The Soothing, Digital Rooms of YouTube," 2021). The demand is structural, not a fad — lo-fi streams read as "one means to navigate collapsed boundaries and the expectation of self-regulation in a control society" (Winston & Saywood, 2019). Since 2024, the gen-AI ambience flood has branded the default look as **AI slop**, and Lofi Girl formally forbids style imitation — which is why the Red Flags below exist.

## Red Flags — STOP if any of these appear

- The prompt directly invokes **"lo-fi girl" / "lofi hip hop girl aesthetic"** → **forbidden.** That composition (desk + girl + cat + window) carries the AI-slop brand, and Lofi Girl officially prohibits style imitation (lofigirl.com/ai). Take the principles (membrane, warmth, lived-in traces); swap the subjects and the composition.
- A 100% orthodox-cliché stack (cat + blanket + rain + vinyl) with **zero strange elements** → one **"slightly strange" element is mandatory** (an out-of-place object, a wrong scale, an unexplained light). It is what separates creation from imitation — and since the genre's core mechanism is that *imperfection carries warmth*, strangeness is an extension of the genre, not a violation.
- **Streaks, visit rewards, accumulation counters, timers** → no matter how softly wrapped ("only dust settles on it"), **a counter is a counter.** Reinvent obligation and you are on the BeReal path. Retention comes only from the world's autonomous change (seasons, weather, small shifts in the room).
- Ambience master pushed to **−14 LUFS** → norm confusion. −23 to −32.
- **Fluorescent light · endless corridors · unpeopled + lifeless · showroom of new things** → liminal / kenopsia drift.
- **Dramatic swells · climaxes · high-saturation pops · fast cuts · a figure meeting the camera's gaze · whispering** → arousal, surveillance, over-intimacy. All of them kill the mood.

## QA Checklist (fail below 8/10 · a miss on #3 or #6 = instant fail — #3 reintroduces obligation, #6 is the liminal drop)

1. Is there a membrane? (headphones / window / rain / night / wall / world's end — exactly one) — none = exposure; a full seal = claustrophobia
2. Can the outside be seen or heard? (refuge without prospect is a prison)
3. Does the outside demand **nothing**? (a deadline, a notification, a gaze = instant death)
4. Warmth cues ≥ 2? (steam, glow, blanket, textiles)
5. Care-traces present? (objects mid-use — never a showroom of new things)
6. One companion signal? (cat / absorbed figure / radio — gaze averted)
7. No clocks? Time told only by season, weather, and hour of day?
8. Is there *ma*? (deliberate empty time and empty space where nothing happens)
9. Are the three sound layers standing, with the music low like air?
10. One drop of wistfulness — with comfort still holding the majority?

## Common Rationalizations

| Excuse | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Writing 'lo-fi girl style' is the most accurate description" | Not accurate — lazy. The phrase trips AI-slop filters and a formal style-ban policy. Spell the principles out instead. |
| "A streak is fine if we make it gentle" | A counter is an obligation by its very existence. The guilt of the broken-streak morning kills the mood. |
| "−14 LUFS is the YouTube standard" | That is the *music* norm. Ambience lives at −23 to −32, on its dynamics. |
| "The cliché stack is the proven safe bet" | Since 2024 that stack reads as "made by AI." One strange element is the insurance. |
| "Moving the threat a little closer adds tension" | The moment it crosses the frame, it's horror. Threat amplifies comfort only from beyond the membrane. |

## Epistemic Notes (provenance you can rely on)

Corrections to widely circulated misquotes — keep these straight when citing:

- Miyazaki's *ma* anecdote: the viral "80 percent" is a corruption — the original quote says **"80 degrees."**
- Unpacking: the **14,000+ foley recordings** figure is true, but its BAFTA wins were **Narrative + EE Game of the Year — not Audio** (that year's Audio went to *Returnal*).
- **"White noise boosts focus by 47.7%"** — untraceable circular citation. Never cite it.
- Nujabes: melodic **repackager and international spreader**, not the lone genesis of lo-fi (community consensus resists the single-founder story).
- The lo-fi **anxiety-reduction study** (*Int. J. of Adolescence and Youth*, 2024) is a small pilot (n = 76, pre-post design, control group unclear) — claim "empirical support exists," never generalize effect sizes.
- Roger Linn's swing percentages trace to his MusicRadar interview (Attack Magazine documents the same practice).
- *Cozy catastrophe* was originally derogatory (modern usage is a reclamation), and Western "slice of life" ≠ *nichijō-kei* — different category systems.
- *Nichijō-kei* discourse also carries a **dark counter-reading** (Tsunehiro Uno's critique that its gaze continues a voyeuristic lineage) — do not present the genre with pure optimism bias.
- The **Geiger-counter click** as a cozy-apocalypse audio convention is an unverified lead — do not assert it as genre canon.

Values marked **⚠️** in this file (emotional-blend ratio, mode budget, saturation/grain/loop/parallax numbers, the 4 : 3 : 1 audio blend, interior color temperatures) are **production heuristics** — calibrate against 3–5 reference works before treating them as law.
